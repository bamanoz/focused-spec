# Proposal

## Why

Repeated blind Kiro revisions stop after editing prose even though the selected layout already requires evidence: the portable reconciliation section addresses only already-enrolled items. Separately, a failed ACP responder loses its output, and a later todo continuation replaces substantive failure-interview recommendations.

## What Changes

- Clarify that revising a native item selected by existing configuration includes resolving missing enrollment, within host authorization; do not enroll unrelated native content.
- Preserve all completed research replies and retain failed or undecodable ACP responder output without manufacturing consent or rescoring historical runs.
- Keep official host workflows, blind prompts, installed-package protection, exact evidence, and sensitivity gates unchanged.
- Verify the repaired handoff through fresh public and native evaluations, preserving failures and unavailable host validation/lifecycle verdicts.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `agent-workflow`: Complete missing enrollment for touched configured native items and retain diagnostic evidence across failed interactions and multi-message research.

## Impact

CLI-owned `guidance/workflow.md`, eval dialogue/interview/ACP transport, their regression coverage, and owning documentation. No CLI grammar, SDK, configuration schema, runner protocol, dependency, release, or consumer integration changes. Sync/archive and publishing remain separate operations.
